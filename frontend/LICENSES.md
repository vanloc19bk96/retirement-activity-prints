## Open Peeps style (`@dicebear/collection` → `openPeeps`)

- **License:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) — verified in `node_modules/@dicebear/open-peeps/LICENSE` (Design section).
- **Artist:** Pablo Stanley (Open Peeps)
- **Use:** The DiceBear avatar style used by Face–Name Association.
- No attribution required. Unlimited commercial use, including embedding in a paid SaaS and selling generated books on Amazon KDP.
- **Why this style:** its heads are drawn as distinct men and women, so gender reads correctly on its own. Lorelei (also CC0) had a single feminine base and could only fake a male by pasting a beard on a feminine face, which looked broken. Open Peeps needs no such hack.

## Lorelei style (`@dicebear/collection` → `lorelei`)

- **License:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
- **Artist:** Lisa Wischofsky
- **Use:** Also CC0 and safe for KDP. No longer used by Face–Name Association (see Open Peeps above).
- No attribution required. Unlimited commercial use.

## Hard rule

Only **CC0** DiceBear styles may back Face–Name Association (currently Open Peeps; Lorelei is also CC0). Do **not** use styles whose design is licensed under CC-BY (for example Adventurer, Micah, Personas, Big Smile, Miniavs, Croodles). Those require visible artist attribution in every sold book, which this product does not support. Never expose a style picker that can select a CC-BY style. Always confirm a style's design license in its `LICENSE` file before using it.
